Life Beyond the Classroom

University life is not only about academics. At LPU, students can participate in cultural activities, competitions, performances and student organisations.

Students from different states and countries come together to share their cultures, ideas and talents. This makes the campus a lively and diverse place.

Cultural Events at LPU

One India

One India celebrates the diversity of Indian culture. Students get the opportunity to showcase the traditions, food, clothing, music and heritage of different regions of India.

  1. one world

    2. One World brings students from different countries and cultures together. It gives students an opportunity to experience international traditions, performances and cultural expressions.


  2. Spectra

    3. Spectra is a youth festival that brings together creativity, talent and cultural performances. Students can participate in different competitions and showcase their skills.
